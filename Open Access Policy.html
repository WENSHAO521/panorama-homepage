Open Access Governance

Open-access framework for distribution, licensing, and rights clarity.

Panorama journals implement open-access terms at journal level under a shared publisher policy baseline covering license disclosure, author rights, and archiving practice.

01

Reader Access

Published research is made available online according to journal-specific access terms.

02

Operating Model

Journals may apply Diamond OA or Gold OA, including APC policies where relevant.

03

License Disclosure

Applicable Creative Commons licensing is identified at journal level.

04

Repository Use

Deposits and archiving should preserve citation integrity of the version of record.

Author Rights

Copyright retention with publication permission terms.

Unless journal agreements specify otherwise, authors retain copyright and grant non-exclusive rights for publication, distribution, indexing, and maintenance of the version of record.
