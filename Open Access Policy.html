PSG Policy Statement

Open Access
Policy.

Supporting broad and lawful access to scholarly work through transparent licensing, responsible dissemination, and sustainable publishing models.

format_quote

"Panorama Scholarly Group (PSG) supports the widest possible dissemination of scholarly research. We believe that open and lawful access to research contributes to academic exchange, public knowledge, and long-term scholarly visibility."

01

Definition

In accordance with widely recognized open access principles, including the Budapest Open Access Initiative (BOAI), Panorama Scholarly Group understands open access as the online availability of scholarly literature without price barriers for readers, while respecting applicable copyright, licensing, and attribution requirements.

02

Article Processing Charges (APC)

Panorama Scholarly Group supports sustainable open access publishing models intended to maintain peer review, editorial processing, production, hosting, and long-term preservation of scholarly content.

diamond

Diamond Open Access

Journal: Silence

For the journal Silence, PSG currently supports a Diamond Open Access model. Under this model, authors are not charged article processing fees and readers may access published content without subscription or paywall barriers.

toll

Gold Open Access

Selected Journals

For other journals within the Panorama Scholarly Group, a Gold Open Access model may apply. In such cases, an Article Processing Charge (APC) may be required following editorial acceptance in order to support editorial handling, production, online hosting, and long-term access services.

Journal-Specific Information: APC levels, waiver availability, discount arrangements, and article-type distinctions are determined at the journal level. Authors should consult the relevant journal’s submission guidelines and policy pages for current information.
03

Licensing Options

PSG journals may publish content under selected Creative Commons licenses. The applicable license is determined by the journal’s policy and, where relevant, the publication agreement associated with the article.

CC BY 4.0
Attribution 4.0 International

Permits sharing, adaptation, and reuse of the work, including for commercial purposes, provided that appropriate attribution is given to the original author(s) and source.

CC BY-NC 4.0
Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0

Permits sharing, adaptation, and reuse for non-commercial purposes, provided that appropriate attribution is given and the reuse complies with the non-commercial restriction.

04

Copyright Retention

Unless otherwise stated by the relevant journal policy or publication agreement, authors retain copyright in their work. By agreeing to publication, authors grant the relevant journal and/or Panorama Scholarly Group a non-exclusive license to publish, distribute, and identify itself as the original publisher of the version of record.

05

Repository Deposit & Archiving

PSG supports the long-term accessibility of scholarly work. Subject to the applicable journal policy and license terms, authors may deposit relevant versions of their work in institutional repositories, disciplinary repositories, or other lawful archiving systems. Policies concerning preprints, accepted manuscripts, and published versions may vary by journal and should be checked in the relevant author guidelines.
