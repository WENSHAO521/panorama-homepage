Publisher Ethics Framework

Baseline ethics requirements for peer-reviewed publication.

This policy defines the minimum standards expected from authors, reviewers, editors, and journal teams across the Panorama portfolio.

01

Editorial Decisions

Decisions are made on scope fit, scholarly merit, and policy compliance.

02

Peer Review Conduct

Review is confidential, objective, and supported by clear scholarly reasoning.

03

Originality Standards

Submissions must be original, accurately cited, and free of undisclosed duplicate publication.

04

Post-Publication Correction

When needed, journals issue corrections, concerns, or retractions to protect the record.

Conflict Disclosure

Disclosure is required where relationships may affect judgment.

Authors, reviewers, editors, and board members should disclose financial, institutional, collaborative, or personal relationships that could reasonably be perceived to influence editorial or review decisions.
