PSG Policy Statement

Publication
Ethics.

Upholding the highest standards of academic integrity, rigorous peer review, and editorial transparency in accordance with recognized global publishing guidelines.

gavel

"Ethical publishing is the foundation of scholarly communication. Panorama Scholarly Group is committed to ensuring that all parties—authors, reviewers, and editors—adhere to strictly defined ethical parameters to maintain the integrity of the academic record."

01

Editorial
Responsibilities

Editors of PSG journals are accountable for everything published in their journals. They must ensure the quality of the material they publish, champion freedom of expression, and maintain the integrity of the academic record.

  • check_circle Editorial decisions are based solely on the manuscript’s intellectual content, independent of the authors' race, gender, sexual orientation, religious belief, ethnic origin, citizenship, or political philosophy.
  • check_circle Editors will not disclose any information about a submitted manuscript to anyone other than the corresponding author, reviewers, and the publisher, as appropriate.
02

Peer Review
Integrity

Peer review is the essential method by which PSG ensures the validity of scholarly publishing. All submitted research articles undergo rigorous peer review by independent, anonymous expert referees.

Reviewer Obligations

Reviewers must treat received manuscripts as confidential documents. They should not use unpublished materials disclosed in a submitted manuscript for their own research without the author's explicit written consent. Reviews should be conducted objectively, and observations should be formulated clearly with supporting arguments so that authors can use them for improving the paper.

03

Originality &
Plagiarism

Authors must ensure that they have written entirely original works. Submitting an Original Text entails full accountability for its intellectual integrity. Plagiarism in all its forms constitutes unethical publishing behavior and is unacceptable.

Prior Rejections and Review History

To assist the editorial board in efficiently evaluating the academic history of a manuscript, authors are encouraged to disclose previous submission histories. When submitting an Original Text, providing screenshots of prior rejections or previous review reports is a strongly recommended practice rather than a mandatory requirement. This transparency accelerates the review process and prevents redundant evaluations.

04

Conflicts of
Interest

All participants in the peer-review and publication process—not only authors but also reviewers, editors, and editorial board members—must consider and disclose their conflicts of interest when fulfilling their roles. Authors must disclose any financial or other substantive conflicts of interest that might be construed to influence the results or interpretation of their manuscript.

05

Corrections &
Retractions

When an author discovers a significant error or inaccuracy in their own published work, it is the author’s obligation to promptly notify the journal editor or publisher and cooperate with the editor to retract or correct the paper. If the editor or the publisher learns from a third party that a published work contains a significant error, PSG will initiate a thorough investigation and, if necessary, publish an erratum, corrigendum, or initiate a retraction in accordance with COPE guidelines.
