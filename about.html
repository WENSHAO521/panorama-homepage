Operating Framework

How the publishing platform is structured.

01

Portfolio Architecture

Titles are grouped into four subject clusters for discovery and editorial routing: Technology & Engineering, Health & Sustainability, Policy, Education & Society, and Humanities & Arts.

02

Editorial Handling

Each journal manages submissions and peer review within its defined scope and standards, with editorial oversight at journal level.

03

Policy Consistency

Shared policy pages establish baseline requirements across the portfolio for publication ethics, open access, and conflict disclosure.

04

Public Access Layer

Journal pages, policy pages, and published articles are publicly available. DOIs are registered with Crossref for persistent citation and discovery.
