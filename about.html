Institutional Overview

An academic publishing initiative.

Panorama Scholarly Group supports peer-reviewed scholarly communication through journal-level editorial activity, public policy pages, and digital publishing infrastructure.

Administrative and operational functions are coordinated through Hong Kong. The publisher maintains policies covering peer review, publication ethics, editorial transparency, and open access in line with recognized scholarly publishing standards.

The publishing model is designed to support durable access to research, interdisciplinary exchange, and responsible participation in the global scholarly communication ecosystem.

Institutional Pillars

Fundamental commitments.

The programme is anchored in editorial responsibility and access to the scholarly record.

01

Editorial Rigor

Supporting editorial assessment and peer review procedures designed to protect research integrity, methodological clarity, and scholarly accountability.

02

Open Access

Promoting public access to scholarly work through open dissemination models that reduce barriers to readership and academic exchange.

03

Interdisciplinary Exchange

Connecting humanities, social sciences, policy studies, engineering, health, sustainability, and emerging technological fields.

04

Scholarly Record

Maintaining discoverable journal pages, issue records, article metadata, corrections, and policy commitments across the publishing programme.

Scholarly Integrity

Committed to the academic record.

PSG maintains publication policies designed to protect the integrity of scholarly communication. Authors, reviewers, editors, and journal teams are expected to participate in a transparent and accountable publication process.
