Our Mission

Making rigorous scholarship openly accessible worldwide.

Panorama Scholarly Group was founded on the conviction that rigorous peer review, open access, and long-term scholarly infrastructure are complementary values. We operate journals, books, research programs, and open scholarly platforms that support transparent and accessible scholarship worldwide.

Journal Catalogue 22 peer-reviewed open-access journals across 4 subject clusters. Scholarly Books Monographs, edited volumes, and book series with full peer review. Editorial Standards Peer review integrity, authorship policy, and correction procedures. Open Access Commitment All publications freely available under Creative Commons licensing.

Publishing Model

How we publish — journals and scholarly books.

01

Catalogue Architecture

Journal titles are grouped into four subject clusters: Technology & Engineering, Health & Sustainability, Policy, Education & Society, and Humanities & Arts. Scholarly books span the same disciplinary range.

02

Editorial Handling

Each journal and book project is managed by a dedicated editorial team within its defined scope and standards. Peer review is conducted at publication level with editorial oversight throughout.

03

Policy Consistency

Shared policy baselines apply across all publications — journals and books — covering publication ethics, open access, conflict disclosure, and post-publication correction.

04

Open Discovery

All published content is publicly accessible. Articles receive Crossref DOIs (prefix 10.63802); content is indexed in Google Scholar and archived in CLOCKSS and the PKP Preservation Network.
