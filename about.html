    Our Mission

    Making rigorous scholarship openly accessible worldwide.

    Panorama Scholarly Group was founded on the idea that peer review and citation infrastructure should be as rigorous and transparent as the research they support. We operate journals, books, research programs, and POSI, an open index that scores journal quality from lifecycle and citation data rather than a single legacy metric.

    Journal Catalogue 24 peer-reviewed open-access journals across five imprints. Scholarly Books Monographs, edited volumes, and book series with full peer review. Editorial Standards Peer review integrity, authorship policy, and correction procedures. Open Access Commitment All publications freely available under Creative Commons licensing.

    Publishing Model

    How we publish — journals and scholarly books.

    01

    Catalogue Architecture

    Journal titles are grouped into five imprints: Ridgeline (technology, engineering & AI), Health Nexus (medicine & health sciences), Verdant Science (environment & life sciences), Charter (policy, law & governance), and Threnody (humanities, arts & philosophy). Scholarly books span the same disciplinary range.

    02

    Editorial Handling

    Each journal and book project is managed by a dedicated editorial team within its defined scope and standards. Peer review is conducted at publication level with editorial oversight throughout.

    03

    Policy Consistency

    Shared policy baselines apply across all publications — journals and books — covering publication ethics, open access, conflict disclosure, and post-publication correction.

    04

    Open Discovery

    All published content is publicly accessible. Articles receive Crossref DOIs (prefix 10.63802); content is indexed in Google Scholar and archived in CLOCKSS and the PKP Preservation Network.
