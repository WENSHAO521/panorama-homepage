Institutional Overview

A Distributed
Academic Publishing
Initiative.

Panorama Scholarly Group (PSG) operates through international digital publishing workflows with a commitment to editorial transparency, responsible peer review, and open scholarly communication.

Selected journals within PSG hold ISSNs assigned through the German ISSN Centre. Such ISSN assignment relates to journal identification and metadata registration, and should not be interpreted as a statement of publisher headquarters, legal domicile, or evaluation database status.

Administrative and operational functions are coordinated through Hong Kong. PSG maintains publication policies covering peer review, publication ethics, editorial transparency, and open access in line with recognized scholarly publishing standards.

Our publishing model is designed to support durable access to research, interdisciplinary exchange, and responsible participation in the global scholarly communication ecosystem.
