Institutional Overview

A Distributed Academic Publishing Initiative.

Panorama Scholarly Group operates through international digital publishing workflows with a commitment to editorial transparency, responsible peer review, and open scholarly communication.

The group is organized around durable access, accountable editorial practice, and participation in the global scholarly communication ecosystem.
