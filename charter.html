About Charter.

Charter is the imprint for public policy, law, education, and the social sciences that study how institutions are governed and held accountable. Its eight journals range from political economy and computational social science to education policy and political thought.

Two of Charter's titles, the Journal of Public Oversight and Institutional Integrity and the Global Governance Systems Review, launched in 2026. The latter continues the governance-scope coverage previously carried by Global Public Policy & Governance Review, now retired.
