# Asset Preservation Layer — Round 1

This document explains `assets-manifest.json` (repo root) and the
preserve/review/archive/reject decisions behind it. The manifest is
generated by [`scripts/build_assets_manifest.py`](../../scripts/build_assets_manifest.py),
which only **reads** the legacy `assets/`, `Homepage Image/`, and
`QKFM/` directories — it never modifies or deletes an original file.
Re-run it any time to refresh the manifest as decisions change.

## Status definitions

- **preserve** — official mark, factual/partner logo, or otherwise
  carries forward unchanged into the new site.
- **review** — usable, but needs one of: licensing confirmation,
  image re-encoding (WebP/AVIF), or an art-direction fit check against
  the Editorial Institutional Modernism direction before reuse.
- **archive** — kept for historical/reference value only; not reused
  in the new build. (None found in this pass — see note below.)
- **reject** — duplicate or superseded; do not carry forward.

## Category breakdown

### `brand/logos`, `brand/imprints` — preserve

The primary Panorama wordmark
(`QKFM/panorama-scholarly-group-uppercase-wordmark-4k.svg`, 1.4 KB) and
the full `assets/logos/` and `assets/nexus/` sets: PSG's own logo,
the five imprint logos (Ridgeline, Health Nexus, Verdant Science,
Charter, Threnody — light and dark variants), POSI, Journals, Books,
Research Institute, and Editorial Directory marks. These are official
brand assets and are preserved as-is per the project brief.

Two items in this set are flagged `review`, not `preserve`:

- `assets/logos/journals-logo.svg` (223 KB) and
  `journals-logo-light.svg` (171 KB) — these are **implausibly large**
  for vector logo marks. They almost certainly contain embedded raster
  data. Needs inspection and a clean re-export before reuse; do not
  ship 223 KB of "SVG" for a nav-bar logo.
- `assets/logos/screen.png` (1600×1600, 39.8 KB) — generic filename,
  no obvious usage found in this pass. Confirm it's still referenced
  before deciding preserve vs. discard.

### `icons/indexers` — preserve, with a caveat

Crossref, DOI Foundation, Dimensions, Google Scholar, ISNI, ISSN,
LOCKSS, CLOCKSS, OpenAIRE, OpenAlex, PKP, Semantic Scholar, ZDB,
Zenodo, BASE, CJWK, and the PLOS OA mark. **These are third-party
trademarks**, not PSG intellectual property. They are preserved for
factual/indexing display (the "we are indexed here" strip), but must
follow each partner's own logo-usage policy — they are never restyled,
recolored, or redrawn to fit PSG's design system.

### `brand/partners` — preserve, one duplicate rejected

`library-logo.png` and the Taoist Association of Korea partner mark.
The Korea mark exists in **two identical copies**:
`assets/logos/taoist-association-korea-logo.png` and
`QKFM/Taoist Association of Korea.png` (both 131.6 KB). The `QKFM`
copy is marked **reject** as a duplicate; the `assets/logos/` copy is
the canonical one going forward.

### `photography/wallpapers` — review

Five 4K (3840×2160) full-bleed imprint wallpapers, 2.1–2.7 MB each
(11.4 MB total). Two open questions, not just an optimization task:

1. **Art-direction fit.** Editorial Institutional Modernism is
   restrained; large photographic full-bleed hero wallpapers are the
   kind of "SaaS hero" treatment this rebuild explicitly steers away
   from (see `DESIGN-FOUNDATION.md`). Round 3 must decide whether these
   survive in any homepage/imprint-hero role, get repurposed as smaller
   supporting imagery, or are retired.
2. **Weight.** At 2+ MB each as JPEG, these fail the performance
   baseline outright regardless of the design decision. If kept in any
   form, they need WebP/AVIF re-encoding and responsive `srcset`
   variants — never shipped as a single 4K JPEG to a mobile client.

### `photography/homepage` — review

Six per-journal hero images (`Homepage Image/`, 1672×941 except one
4K JPEG), 0.86–2.3 MB each. Same two questions as the wallpapers:
licensing confirmation and a fit check against the new visual
language, plus mandatory re-encoding.

### `imprints/covers` — review

24 journal-cover-style graphics in `QKFM/` (495×700, i.e. a book/cover
aspect ratio), 200–620 KB each as PNG — collectively the single
largest optimization opportunity in the whole asset set relative to
their visual complexity. A cover graphic at that resolution has no
reason to weigh 600 KB. Confirm copyright ownership per journal, then
re-encode.

### `photography/editorial` — review

`QKFM/Scholarly_Integrity.jpg` (1000×667, 58.9 KB) — editorial stock
photography used on ethics/integrity content. Confirm license terms.

### `brand/favicon` — preserve

`favicon.svg` and `favicon.png` (512×512, per `manifest.json`).
Already copied into `web/public/` as part of the Round 1 shell so the
foundation prototype resolves a real favicon instead of a 404.

## What "REFERENCE ONLY" material is *not* in this manifest

Per the project brief, journal data, organisation facts, and policy
text are **reference source material**, not assets. That content lives
in `data/*.json` (journals, announcements, articles, POSI stats) and
across the legacy HTML bodies, and is out of scope for this manifest.
It is the input to the **content model** work in Round 2, not to the
asset pipeline.

## Why nothing is `archive` yet

Round 1's brief is explicitly *audit, don't delete*. Nothing here has
been judged historical-only — every non-rejected file is either
straightforwardly reusable (`preserve`) or reusable-pending-a-decision
(`review`). `archive` will start being used once Round 3/4 make actual
keep/drop calls on the `review` items (e.g., if the wallpapers are
dropped from the new hero treatment, they move to `archive` rather
than being deleted, in case a later round wants them for a different
purpose).

## Known gap in this pass

Per-page image usage was **not** cross-referenced against every legacy
HTML file in this round (the `usage` field in the manifest says
`unaudited-per-page`). The working tree currently holds an uncommitted
change (see Risk R1 in the main report) that already reduced every
legacy page's `<body>` to just its `<img>` tags — which is exactly the
per-page usage signal this manifest would want, but its provenance is
unconfirmed, so it was not relied on here. Round 2 should redo this
cross-reference deliberately, against a known-good source (`git show
HEAD:<file>` for each page, or the working tree once its status is
resolved).
