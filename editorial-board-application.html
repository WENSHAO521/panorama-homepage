Join Editorial Board

Become an Editorial Board Member.

Panorama Scholarly Group's journals invite scholars worldwide to join their editorial boards. We welcome researchers with strong academic backgrounds and peer-review experience to help uphold the quality of international scholarly publishing.

How to Apply

Materials Required
Curriculum Vitae (CV)
Send To
contact@panorama-sg.com
or the journal's editorial office — see table below
Email Subject
Editorial Board Application — [Journal Name]
Response Time
Within 4 weeks of receipt

Application Requirements

Eligibility criteria and responsibilities.

Eligibility

Applicants should hold a doctoral degree or equivalent academic qualification, with a track record of peer-reviewed publications in a relevant field.

Applicants should have independent research experience and be familiar with current developments in their discipline.

Experience providing peer review for international academic journals is preferred.

Responsibilities

Provide expert peer review for submitted manuscripts within the board member's area of expertise, offering fair and constructive evaluations.

Assist the editor-in-chief in upholding the journal's academic quality standards and ensuring published content meets disciplinary norms.

Optionally participate in special issue planning, calls for papers, and promoting the journal within your institution and academic community.

Application Materials

Please include: full name, institutional affiliation, title, research focus, representative publications (with DOI or link), and peer review history.

Please state in your email the journal(s) you wish to join. If unsure, browse the journal directory to find the best match for your research.

Submit your CV as a PDF or Word document (max 10 MB) as an email attachment.

Contact the Editorial Office

contact@panorama-sg.com

Editorial Board Application — [Journal Name], e.g. Editorial Board Application — Journal of Engineering Systems & Applications

All applications are treated in confidence. There is no application fee or membership charge for editorial board positions.
