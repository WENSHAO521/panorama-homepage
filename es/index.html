Convocatoria de artículos: Resonance diciembre 2026 — Sonidos en circulación

Resonance: Revista de Estudios Musicales Globales anuncia la convocatoria de artículos para su número de diciembre de 2026, titulado «Sonidos en circulación: traducción, mediación y públicos en los estudios musicales globales». El consejo editorial invita a contribuciones académicas originales que examinen cómo la música atraviesa fronteras culturales, lingüísticas e institucionales mediante procesos de traducción, mediación tecnológica e implicación pública. Se aceptan envíos en inglés, chino, alemán, italiano y ruso; todos los manuscritos deben incluir título y resumen en inglés. Un APC promocional de USD 140 se aplica a los envíos realizados en 2026.

Frank Gunderson Joins Resonance as Academic Advisor

Resonance: Journal of Global Music Studies is pleased to announce that Professor Frank Gunderson has joined the journal as an Academic Advisor. Frank Gunderson is Professor of Musicology at Florida State University. He received the B.A. degree from Evergreen State College (WA), and the M.A. in World Music and Ph.D. in Ethnomusicology from Wesleyan University (CT). He has held previous appointments at Ohio University and University of Michigan, and he once taught for two years in a village Harambee secondary school in Kenya. Gunderson’s research and teaching interests include musical intersections with Intangible Cultural Heritage (ICH) and sonic repatriation, African history, Islam, musical labor, veterans’ issues, biographical approaches, refugee communities, and documentary film. His research has been supported recently by Fulbright (Distinguished Chair Award, 2021-2022), and he has received awards from the National Endowment for the Humanities, Fulbright-Hays, and the Wenner-Gren Foundation. Gunderson’s professional affiliations include the African Studies Association (ASA), the American Anthropological Association (AAA), and the Society for Ethnomusicology (SEM). Gunderson was recently General Editor (2018-2022) of the SEM academic journal Ethnomusicology and is co-founder and co-Editor-in-Chief (together with Benjamin Harbert) of the new SEM Journal of Audiovisual Ethnomusicology. He has also served as the SEM Journal’s Film, Video, and Multimedia Review Editor, and has twice been a guest editor of the journal World of Music. Gunderson has published more than fifty articles and reviews in Ethnomusicology, Africa Today, History and Anthropology, Soundings, and African Music, many of which can be found here. He has conducted extensive fieldwork in East Africa, and produced the recording Tanzania: Farmer Composers of North West Tanzania (1997, Multicultural Media) and co-edited with Gregory Barz the book Mashindano!: Competitive Music Performance in East Africa (2000, Mkuki na Nyota Press/African Books Collective LTD, Michigan State University Press). His book “We Never Sleep We Dream of Farming”: Sukuma Labor Songs from Western Tanzania (Brill Academic Press, 2010) was the 2009-2011 winner of the SEM Kwabena Nketia Book Award for best African music monograph. He co-edited with Robert Lancefield and Bret Woods The Oxford Handbook of Musical Repatriation (OUP 2018), and recently completed the dual biography, The Legacy of Tanzanian Musicians Muhidin Gurumo and Hassan Bitchuka – Rhumba Kiserebuka! (2018, Lexington Books/Rowman and Littlefield), which received Honorable Mentions from both the Alan Meriam Best Book prize committee (SEM) and the Best Book Prize committee of the International Council for Traditional Music. Gunderson is an award-winning documentary filmmaker. While music director of KAOS radio in Olympia, WA, he worked with his cousin, the Northwest “outsider” musician Travis Roberts, on a project that culminated in several high-profile indie recordings as well as an award-winning film co-directed with Bret Woods called Human Skab (SnagFilms, 2012). His recent film about a Tanzanian drum-maker, Ng’oma: The Work of Our Clan (Alexander Street / Ethnographic Video Online, 2020), has been shown in dozens of film festivals worldwide. His most recent film project, “Beloved Youth of Many Days”: Stories about Mlimani Park Orchestra, has been accepted into fifty international film festivals and is distributed by the Royal Anthropological Institute. Resonance: Journal of Global Music Studies is committed to fostering cross-cultural, transregional, and interdisciplinary dialogue in global music studies. Professor Gunderson’s experience in ethnomusicology, African music research, sonic repatriation, audiovisual scholarship, musical labor, and academic publishing will provide valuable guidance as the journal continues to develop its scholarly community. We warmly welcome Professor Frank Gunderson to the Academic Advisory Board of Resonance.

Call for Papers: Volume 1 of Contemporary Review of Political Thought

Contemporary Review of Political Thought (CRoPT) is now inviting submissions for its first volume. The journal welcomes original scholarly work in English or Chinese. Contemporary Review of Political Thought（CRoPT，《当代政治思想评论》）现面向学界征集第一卷稿件。本刊欢迎英文或中文原创学术论文投稿。 CRoPT is an international, peer-reviewed, open-access online academic journal dedicated to the study of political thought, political philosophy, intellectual history, Marxist theory, ideological and political education, higher education governance, and modern public life. CRoPT 是一本国际性、同行评审、开放获取的在线学术期刊，关注政治思想、政治哲学、智识史、马克思主义理论、思想政治教育、高等教育治理与现代公共生活等领域。 We welcome submissions that engage with political concepts, theoretical traditions, institutional change, public values, democracy, justice, power, sovereignty, freedom, equality, rights, citizenship, public reason, legitimacy, modernity, globalization, social movements, public life, technological change, and contemporary political imagination. 本刊欢迎围绕政治概念、思想传统、制度变迁、公共价值、民主、正义、权力、主权、自由、平等、权利、公民身份、公共理性、合法性、现代性、全球化、社会运动、公共生活、技术变迁与当代政治想象等主题展开的研究。 投稿稿件须为原创、未发表且未一稿多投。稿件建议使用可编辑 Word 文件，并包含摘要、关键词、正文、注释、参考文献及必要的表格、图示、附录和相关声明。 Research articles, theoretical essays, review articles, policy and institutional analyses, and special issue articles are normally subject to double-blind peer review. Forum contributions, book reviews, invited pieces, and shorter texts may be assessed through editorial review, peer review, or a mixed process, depending on the nature of the submission. 研究论文、理论论文、综述论文、政策与制度分析及专题论文通常采用双盲同行评审。论坛文章、书评、约稿或短文可根据稿件性质采用编辑评审、同行评审或混合评审流程。 Authors are invited to submit manuscripts through the journal’s online submission system. Please read the Author Guidelines before submission and ensure that all required metadata and declarations are completed accurately. 作者请通过本刊在线投稿系统提交稿件。投稿前请阅读作者指南，并确保完整、准确填写稿件元数据及相关声明。 Submissions that do not fit the journal’s aims and scope, lack required information, or raise unresolved ethical concerns may be returned to authors or declined during initial editorial screening. 明显不符合本刊范围、材料不完整，或存在未解决伦理问题的稿件，可能在编辑初筛阶段被退回或拒绝。

Contemporary Review of Political Thought is published annually. Call for Papers for the September Issue: AI, Climate Sustainability, and Environmental Transformation

Climate Sustainability & Global Systems (CSGS) invites submissions for its regular September issue. As artificial intelligence continues to develop rapidly, new opportunities are emerging to improve climate research, environmental monitoring, sustainability transitions, and global systems governance. At the same time, AI applications in climate and environmental research require methodological transparency, ethical responsibility, data integrity, reproducibility, and careful consideration of social, ecological, and policy implications. For the September issue, CSGS particularly welcomes high-quality submissions that explore how responsible AI, environmental data science, modeling, and digital technologies can contribute to climate sustainability, environmental protection, resilience building, and systemic risk analysis. Topics of interest include, but are not limited to: • AI applications in climate modeling, Earth system analysis, and environmental prediction• Machine learning for environmental monitoring, pollution assessment, and ecosystem protection• AI-assisted approaches to climate risk assessment, adaptation planning, and resilience building• Data-driven tools for carbon management, energy transitions, and sustainable infrastructure• Responsible AI for sustainability governance, climate policy, and decision-making• Remote sensing, geospatial analysis, and AI for land, water, biodiversity, and urban systems• AI-based early warning systems for extreme weather, disasters, and systemic climate risks• Socio-ecological impacts of AI-enabled climate and environmental technologies• Equity, justice, transparency, and accountability in AI-driven sustainability solutions• Interdisciplinary frameworks linking AI, climate science, sustainability transitions, and global governance In addition to AI-related submissions, the journal continues to welcome manuscripts across its broader scope, including climate systems modeling, sustainability governance, global climate policy, socio-ecological resilience, climate-economy systems, and interdisciplinary methods and analytical frameworks. CSGS welcomes original research articles, review articles, policy analysis papers, methods and frameworks papers, short communications, and data- or AI-focused reports that align with the journal’s aims and scope. All submissions should present rigorous methods, clear academic English, transparent data and code practices where applicable, and strong relevance to climate sustainability and global systems. Authors using AI tools in text preparation, data processing, coding, modeling, visualization, or image generation must include an appropriate AI Use Statement in the manuscript. Data Availability, Funding, and Conflict of Interest statements are also required. Manuscripts should be submitted through the journal’s official submission system. Authors are encouraged to review the Author Guidelines, Data Policy, AI & Research Integrity Policy, and Publication Ethics Requirements before submission. For inquiries, please contact the Editorial Office at: csgs@panorama-sg.com Climate Sustainability & Global Systems looks forward to receiving rigorous, transparent, and globally relevant research that advances understanding and action at the intersection of climate sustainability, environmental transformation, and global systems.

Call for Editorial Board Members

Climate Sustainability & Global Systems (CSGS) invites expressions of interest from qualified scholars who wish to serve as Editorial Board Members. CSGS is an international, peer-reviewed, open access journal dedicated to interdisciplinary research at the intersection of climate science, sustainability transitions, and global systems governance. The journal welcomes contributions related to climate systems and Earth system interactions, sustainability transitions, environmental data science and AI applications, global climate policy and systemic risk analysis, socio-ecological resilience and adaptation, and interdisciplinary methods and analytical frameworks. We welcome applications from researchers with strong academic backgrounds in one or more of the following areas: • Climate science and Earth systems• Sustainability transitions and governance• Global climate policy and systemic risk• Environmental data science, AI, and modeling• Climate-economy and socio-technical systems• Socio-ecological resilience, adaptation, justice, and global development• Interdisciplinary methods and analytical frameworks Editorial Board Members are expected to support the journal by contributing to one or more of the following activities: • Advising on the academic development and international outreach of the journal• Recommending high-quality submissions and special issue topics• Supporting peer review when manuscripts fall within their expertise• Promoting ethical, rigorous, transparent, and globally relevant scholarship• Helping strengthen the journal’s engagement with diverse research communities Applicants should normally have: • A doctoral degree or equivalent research experience in a relevant field• A strong publication record in climate, sustainability, environmental systems, governance, policy, data science, or related interdisciplinary research• Experience in peer review, editorial service, research leadership, or scholarly community engagement• A commitment to publication ethics, academic integrity, and constructive peer review Interested scholars are invited to send the following materials to the Editorial Office: 1. A brief expression of interest2. A current CV3. Research areas and keywords4. A short statement describing how the applicant could contribute to CSGS5. Institutional affiliation and contact information Please send applications or inquiries to: csgs@panorama-sg.com Applications will be reviewed by the editorial leadership of CSGS. Appointment decisions will be based on academic expertise, editorial experience, disciplinary fit, geographic and institutional diversity, and alignment with the journal’s aims and ethical standards. Climate Sustainability & Global Systems looks forward to working with scholars committed to advancing rigorous, transparent, and globally relevant research for climate sustainability and global systems.

Infraestructura abierta

Una base de datos abierta y en crecimiento de calificaciones, clasificaciones y análisis de citas de revistas — construida sobre evidencia versionada y reproducible.

Visitar POSI →

El Índice Académico Abierto Panorama (POSI) se ha convertido en una base de datos abierta de evidencia a nivel de revista: calificaciones basadas en el ciclo de vida (AJR-E para revistas emergentes, AJR-M para revistas consolidadas), clasificaciones temáticas en 48 categorías PSC y análisis de citas (PCI, PCI-5, PNCI). Cada calificación, fuente de evidencia y cálculo está versionado y es reproducible de forma independiente: nada se puntúa a puerta cerrada.

POSI integra datos de Crossref, OpenAlex, OpenCitations, DOAJ, ROR y ORCID, sintetizándolos en un índice de cobertura de revistas, calificaciones e impacto de citas de consulta abierta.
