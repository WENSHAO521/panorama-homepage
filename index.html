For Authors

From journal fit to submission workflow.

The author path is intentionally direct. Choose a journal, check access and ethics requirements, then submit through the selected journal portal.

Step 01Select a journalBrowse by title, discipline, abbreviation, ISSN, or A-Z index. Step 02Review access termsConfirm licensing, copyright, repository deposit, and journal policy. Step 03Prepare declarationsConfirm authorship, originality, conflicts, data, and ethics obligations. Step 04Submit through the journalUse the selected journal route for manuscript submission and current issue access.
