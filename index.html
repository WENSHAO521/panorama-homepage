For Authors

A direct route from journal selection to publication decision.

Author services are presented as a sequence of institutional checks: journal fit, access terms, ethics declarations, and manuscript submission through the journal portal.

Step 01Select a journalBrowse by discipline, title, abbreviation, or ISSN. Step 02Review access termsConfirm licensing, author rights, and repository expectations. Step 03Prepare declarationsConfirm authorship, ethics, conflicts, and data responsibilities. Step 04Submit through the journalContinue from the selected journal to the manuscript workflow.
