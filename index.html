Open Infrastructure

A growing open database of journal ratings, rankings, and citation analytics — built from versioned, reproducible evidence.

Visit POSI →

The Panorama Open Scholarly Index (POSI) has grown into an open database of journal-level evidence: lifecycle-based ratings (AJR-E for early-stage journals, AJR-M for mature ones), subject rankings across 48 PSC categories, and citation analytics (PCI, PCI-5, PNCI). Every rating, evidence source, and calculation is versioned and independently reproducible — nothing is scored behind closed doors.

POSI integrates data from Crossref, OpenAlex, OpenCitations, DOAJ, ROR, and ORCID, synthesising them into an openly queryable index of journal coverage, ratings, and citation impact.
