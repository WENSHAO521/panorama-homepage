    Digital Object Identifiers

    Persistent identifiers registered through Crossref.

    01

    Crossref Membership

    Panorama Scholarly Group is a Crossref member publisher. All accepted articles receive a DOI registered in the Crossref metadata database.

    02

    DOI Prefix

    The platform DOI prefix is 10.63802. Individual journal DOIs follow the format 10.63802/[journal-id].[volume].[issue].[article-id].

    03

    Persistent Resolution

    All DOIs resolve to the article landing page at journals.panorama-sg.com via the DOI Foundation resolver at doi.org.

    04

    Metadata Standards

    Article metadata including title, authors, abstract, and references is deposited with Crossref at the time of publication for downstream discovery and citation.

    DOIs enable persistent citation of the published version of record. Authors and institutions citing Panorama Scholarly Group articles should use the assigned DOI rather than the direct URL to ensure long-term link integrity.

    Discovery Databases

    Journals indexed in major scholarly search and discovery platforms.

    Google Scholar Article-level indexing via Google's scholarly search engine.
    BASE Bielefeld Academic Search Engine — one of the largest academic search engines worldwide.
    Dimensions Research intelligence platform for scholarly discovery and analytics.
    OpenAlex Open catalog of global scholarly metadata and citation networks.
    Semantic Scholar AI-powered academic search engine from the Allen Institute for AI.
    Crossref Official DOI registration and metadata for all published articles.
    CASHL China Academic Social Sciences and Humanities Library — a national document-delivery and discovery platform for humanities and social science literature serving Chinese universities.
    CJWK Changjiang Wenku (长江文库) — a comprehensive knowledge service platform providing cross-disciplinary academic journals and literature.
    OpenAIRE European Commission-backed open access aggregator that harvests and links publication metadata to support open science and funder compliance.

    Preservation & Archiving

    Long-term preservation through distributed archiving networks.

    CLOCKSS Controlled LOCKSS network providing dark archive preservation for journals in the event of publisher discontinuation.
    LOCKSS Distributed digital preservation network enabling participating libraries to preserve their own copies of content.
    PKP Preservation Network A LOCKSS-based preservation network operated by the Public Knowledge Project, designed specifically for journals on the editorial platform.
    Zenodo Open research repository operated by CERN, enabling open deposit and citation of research outputs.

    Panorama Scholarly Group journals participate in the CLOCKSS, LOCKSS, and PKP Preservation Network archiving programs. In the event of publisher discontinuation or content unavailability, archived content will be released by the CLOCKSS network to ensure continued access.

    Authors depositing preprints or accepted manuscripts in Zenodo or institutional repositories should follow the version and citation guidance in the Open Access Policy to preserve the integrity of the version of record.

    Registration & Identifiers

    International registration bodies and identifier systems.

    ISSN International Standard Serial Number registered with the ISSN International Centre for each qualifying journal title.
    ISNI International Standard Name Identifier for publisher and contributor identity management.
    Deutsche Nationalbibliothek German national bibliography providing metadata registration for open access publications.
    DOI Foundation The international body governing the Digital Object Identifier system used for article persistent identifiers.
    ZDB Zeitschriftendatenbank — the German-Austrian union catalogue of serials, assigning each journal a unique ZDB-ID used by academic libraries.

    Journals with registered ISSNs have been verified by the ISSN International Centre. Journals with pending ISSN status are in the registration process. Authors requiring ISSN confirmation should check the individual journal page or contact the publisher directly.
