Core Rules

Open-access requirements used across the portfolio.

2.1

Public access principle

Published research is made available online under each journal's open-access policy and publication agreement.

2.2

Journal-level OA model

Journals may apply different operating models. Authors should confirm APC, waiver, or no-APC conditions before final submission.

2.3

License disclosure

Applicable licenses are communicated at journal level. Authors should verify permitted reuse before publication.

2.4

Author rights and permissions

Unless agreements specify otherwise, authors retain key rights while granting non-exclusive publication and distribution permissions.

2.5

Repository and archiving

Repository deposit should follow journal terms and preserve accurate citation of the version of record.

2.6

Third-party content

Authors are responsible for securing permissions for third-party figures, tables, or images where required by license or copyright law.

2.7

Version clarity

Where multiple versions are posted, records should clearly identify accepted manuscript versus final published version.
