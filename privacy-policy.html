    Privacy Policy

    How we handle information submitted through this website.

    This policy covers panorama-sg.com only. It explains what little information this site collects, why, and who it is shared with. Last updated 15 August 2026.

    At a Glance

    Accounts
    None on this site — no login or registration
    Tracking
    No cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers
    Forms
    Contact form only, sent via a third-party relay

    Details

    What this site collects and how it is used.

    1.1

    Scope of this policy

    This policy covers the panorama-sg.com corporate website only. Panorama Scholarly Group's other platforms — journal submission portals at journals.panorama-sg.com, books.panorama-sg.com, research.panorama-sg.com, posi.panorama-sg.com, profiles.panorama-sg.com, and credentials.panorama-sg.com — are separate systems that may collect information (such as author accounts, manuscripts, or reviewer records) under their own privacy practices.

    1.2

    Contact form

    The form on our Contact page collects the name, email address, institutional affiliation, enquiry type, and message you choose to submit. Submission is voluntary and used only to respond to your enquiry. The form is delivered through FormSubmit.co, a third-party form-processing service that relays the submission to contact@panorama-sg.com by email; we do not otherwise store submissions in a database.

    1.3

    Language preference

    If you switch the display language, your choice is saved locally in your browser's storage (localStorage) so the site remembers it on your next visit. This preference stays on your device and is never transmitted to us.

    1.4

    No accounts, cookies, or trackers

    This website does not require an account, does not set tracking cookies, and does not use analytics or advertising services. We do not sell or share information with third parties for marketing purposes.

    1.5

    Third-party fonts

    Pages load web fonts from Google Fonts. Loading a font makes a request to Google's servers, which may log standard technical information such as IP address, under Google's own privacy practices.

    1.6

    Your choices

    To ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you have submitted through the contact form, email contact@panorama-sg.com.

    1.7

    Children's privacy

    This site is intended for an academic and professional audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

    1.8

    Changes to this policy

    We may update this page from time to time to reflect changes in how the site works. The date above reflects the most recent update.
