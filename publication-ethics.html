    Publication Ethics

    Core ethics requirements for submission, review, and publication.

    These requirements apply across Panorama Scholarly Group journals. Individual journals may publish additional discipline-specific instructions.

    Policy Scope

    Applies To
    Authors, reviewers, editors, and editorial boards
    Core Areas
    Originality, review conduct, disclosures, and corrections
    Outcome
    Integrity of the scholarly record

    Core Rules

    Ethics requirements used in editorial handling.

    1.1

    Editorial decisions

    Editorial decisions are based on journal scope, scholarly merit, and policy compliance, independent of personal or institutional bias.

    1.2

    Peer review conduct

    Reviewers are expected to provide objective, evidence-based assessments and maintain manuscript confidentiality throughout review.

    1.3

    Originality and citation

    Submissions must be original, properly cited, and free from undisclosed duplicate or redundant publication.

    1.4

    Conflict disclosure

    Authors, reviewers, and editors should disclose relationships that could reasonably be seen as influencing editorial judgment.

    1.5

    Corrections and retractions

    When significant issues are confirmed after publication, journals may issue corrections, expressions of concern, or retractions as appropriate.

    1.6

    Confidentiality and data use

    Unpublished manuscript content should not be used for personal research, teaching, or distribution without explicit permission from the rights holder.

    1.7

    Appeals and concerns

    Authors may submit a reasoned appeal where they identify a substantive procedural or evaluative issue in editorial handling.
