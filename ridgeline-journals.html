Panorama Scholarly Group/Ridgeline/Journals

Three journals, one publishing standard.

Ridgeline groups three independently edited journals under a shared imprint identity and a shared set of publishing standards. Each keeps its own aims and scope, its own editorial board, and its own official journal site.

Panorama Frontier Review, AI & Future Society, and the Journal of Engineering Systems & Applications sit under Ridgeline because their subject matter — applied technology, engineering and artificial intelligence — shares an editorial sensibility: a preference for work with a working implementation, a measured outcome, or a rigorously specified proposal. That shared sensibility does not make them one journal. Each title has its own aims and scope statement, its own editorial board making independent acceptance decisions, and its own official website hosted on Panorama Scholarly Group's OJS platform, where manuscripts are actually submitted and reviewed. What Ridgeline adds is a single point of discovery for readers and authors working across this territory, and a shared publishing infrastructure — peer review framework, open-access licensing, DOI registration, indexing outreach and preservation — described in full on About and Standards.

How the Portfolio Is Organized

Three journals, three distinct scopes.

PFR

Multidisciplinary Frontier Scholarship

Panorama Frontier Review takes the broadest remit of the three, publishing original research and analytical scholarship on emerging issues and interdisciplinary debates relevant to Ridgeline's readership.

AFS

AI Ethics & Governance

AI & Future Society is scoped specifically to AI ethics, governance, regulation, human-AI interaction, and the societal transformations driven by intelligent technologies.

JESA

Engineering Systems & Applications

The Journal of Engineering Systems & Applications publishes peer-reviewed interdisciplinary research in engineering systems, applied sciences, and technology-driven solutions for industry and society.
