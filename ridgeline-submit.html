Submit to Ridgeline.

Choose the journal whose aims and scope best match your manuscript. All three Ridgeline journals use continuous, rolling submission, so there is no fixed issue deadline to wait for.

Each Ridgeline journal reviews and accepts manuscripts independently, through its own editorial board and its own submission portal — there is no single Ridgeline-wide queue. The right first step is matching the manuscript's subject to the journal whose published aims and scope best describe it, using the summaries below or the fuller Journals index. Submission itself happens on each journal's own site, part of Panorama Scholarly Group's OJS platform, where manuscript files, metadata and required declarations are uploaded directly to that journal's editorial office.

Not sure where to submit?

If a manuscript could plausibly fit more than one Ridgeline journal, or its subject sits outside all three descriptions above, two options: explore all Ridgeline journals for the fuller scope statements, or contact the editorial team directly with a short description of the work.
