About Ridgeline.

Ridgeline covers the applied side of technology and engineering: systems that get built, deployed, and measured, and the artificial intelligence research that increasingly sits inside them. The imprint favors work with a working implementation over purely theoretical proposals, though both have a place across its three journals.

Each Ridgeline journal runs its own editorial board of practicing engineers and computer scientists, and each follows the group's shared standards for peer review, open licensing, and post-publication correction.
