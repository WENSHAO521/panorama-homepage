Engineering ideas into scholarly impact.

Ridgeline is Panorama Scholarly Group's imprint for applied technology, engineering systems, and artificial intelligence. It is home to three independently edited journals, spanning multidisciplinary frontier scholarship, AI ethics and governance, and engineering systems and applications, each with its own editorial board and its own review process. What the three titles share is a common publishing framework: structured peer review, open-access licensing under Creative Commons, Crossref DOI registration, and long-term digital preservation through CLOCKSS and the PKP Preservation Network. Ridgeline welcomes submissions describing work that has been built, deployed, measured or formally modeled, from researchers and practitioners working anywhere in the world.

    Why Publish with Ridgeline
