    Приём статей: Resonance, декабрь 2026 — «Циркулирующие звуки»

    Resonance: Журнал глобальных музыкальных исследований объявляет о приёме статей для декабрьского выпуска 2026 года на тему «Циркулирующие звуки: перевод, медиация и публики в глобальных музыкальных исследованиях». Редакционная коллегия приглашает оригинальные академические работы, посвящённые тому, как музыка преодолевает культурные, языковые и институциональные границы посредством перевода, технологической медиации и взаимодействия с публикой. Принимаются рукописи на английском, китайском, немецком, итальянском и русском языках; для всех рукописей обязательны английские название и аннотация. Акционный APC для рукописей 2026 года составляет USD 140.

    Frank Gunderson Joins Resonance as Academic Advisor

    Resonance: Journal of Global Music Studies is pleased to announce that Professor Frank Gunderson has joined the journal as an Academic Advisor. Frank Gunderson is Professor of Musicology at Florida State University. He received the B.A. degree from Evergreen State College (WA), and the M.A. in World Music and Ph.D. in Ethnomusicology from Wesleyan University (CT). He has held previous appointments at Ohio University and University of Michigan, and he once taught for two years in a village Harambee secondary school in Kenya. Gunderson’s research and teaching interests include musical intersections with Intangible Cultural Heritage (ICH) and sonic repatriation, African history, Islam, musical labor, veterans’ issues, biographical approaches, refugee communities, and documentary film. His research has been supported recently by Fulbright (Distinguished Chair Award, 2021-2022), and he has received awards from the National Endowment for the Humanities, Fulbright-Hays, and the Wenner-Gren Foundation. Gunderson’s professional affiliations include the African Studies Association (ASA), the American Anthropological Association (AAA), and the Society for Ethnomusicology (SEM). Gunderson was recently General Editor (2018-2022) of the SEM academic journal Ethnomusicology and is co-founder and co-Editor-in-Chief (together with Benjamin Harbert) of the new SEM Journal of Audiovisual Ethnomusicology. He has also served as the SEM Journal’s Film, Video, and Multimedia Review Editor, and has twice been a guest editor of the journal World of Music. Gunderson has published more than fifty articles and reviews in Ethnomusicology, Africa Today, History and Anthropology, Soundings, and African Music, many of which can be found here. He has conducted extensive fieldwork in East Africa, and produced the recording Tanzania: Farmer Composers of North West Tanzania (1997, Multicultural Media) and co-edited with Gregory Barz the book Mashindano!: Competitive Music Performance in East Africa (2000, Mkuki na Nyota Press/African Books Collective LTD, Michigan State University Press). His book “We Never Sleep We Dream of Farming”: Sukuma Labor Songs from Western Tanzania (Brill Academic Press, 2010) was the 2009-2011 winner of the SEM Kwabena Nketia Book Award for best African music monograph. He co-edited with Robert Lancefield and Bret Woods The Oxford Handbook of Musical Repatriation (OUP 2018), and recently completed the dual biography, The Legacy of Tanzanian Musicians Muhidin Gurumo and Hassan Bitchuka – Rhumba Kiserebuka! (2018, Lexington Books/Rowman and Littlefield), which received Honorable Mentions from both the Alan Meriam Best Book prize committee (SEM) and the Best Book Prize committee of the International Council for Traditional Music. Gunderson is an award-winning documentary filmmaker. While music director of KAOS radio in Olympia, WA, he worked with his cousin, the Northwest “outsider” musician Travis Roberts, on a project that culminated in several high-profile indie recordings as well as an award-winning film co-directed with Bret Woods called Human Skab (SnagFilms, 2012). His recent film about a Tanzanian drum-maker, Ng’oma: The Work of Our Clan (Alexander Street / Ethnographic Video Online, 2020), has been shown in dozens of film festivals worldwide. His most recent film project, “Beloved Youth of Many Days”: Stories about Mlimani Park Orchestra, has been accepted into fifty international film festivals and is distributed by the Royal Anthropological Institute. Resonance: Journal of Global Music Studies is committed to fostering cross-cultural, transregional, and interdisciplinary dialogue in global music studies. Professor Gunderson’s experience in ethnomusicology, African music research, sonic repatriation, audiovisual scholarship, musical labor, and academic publishing will provide valuable guidance as the journal continues to develop its scholarly community. We warmly welcome Professor Frank Gunderson to the Academic Advisory Board of Resonance.

    Call for Papers: Volume 1 of Contemporary Review of Political Thought

    Contemporary Review of Political Thought (CRoPT) is now inviting submissions for its first volume. The journal welcomes original scholarly work in English or Chinese. Contemporary Review of Political Thought（CRoPT，《当代政治思想评论》）现面向学界征集第一卷稿件。本刊欢迎英文或中文原创学术论文投稿。 CRoPT is an international, peer-reviewed, open-access online academic journal dedicated to the study of political thought, political philosophy, intellectual history, Marxist theory, ideological and political education, higher education governance, and modern public life. CRoPT 是一本国际性、同行评审、开放获取的在线学术期刊，关注政治思想、政治哲学、智识史、马克思主义理论、思想政治教育、高等教育治理与现代公共生活等领域。 We welcome submissions that engage with political concepts, theoretical traditions, institutional change, public values, democracy, justice, power, sovereignty, freedom, equality, rights, citizenship, public reason, legitimacy, modernity, globalization, social movements, public life, technological change, and contemporary political imagination. 本刊欢迎围绕政治概念、思想传统、制度变迁、公共价值、民主、正义、权力、主权、自由、平等、权利、公民身份、公共理性、合法性、现代性、全球化、社会运动、公共生活、技术变迁与当代政治想象等主题展开的研究。 投稿稿件须为原创、未发表且未一稿多投。稿件建议使用可编辑 Word 文件，并包含摘要、关键词、正文、注释、参考文献及必要的表格、图示、附录和相关声明。 Research articles, theoretical essays, review articles, policy and institutional analyses, and special issue articles are normally subject to double-blind peer review. Forum contributions, book reviews, invited pieces, and shorter texts may be assessed through editorial review, peer review, or a mixed process, depending on the nature of the submission. 研究论文、理论论文、综述论文、政策与制度分析及专题论文通常采用双盲同行评审。论坛文章、书评、约稿或短文可根据稿件性质采用编辑评审、同行评审或混合评审流程。 Authors are invited to submit manuscripts through the journal’s online submission system. Please read the Author Guidelines before submission and ensure that all required metadata and declarations are completed accurately. 作者请通过本刊在线投稿系统提交稿件。投稿前请阅读作者指南，并确保完整、准确填写稿件元数据及相关声明。 Submissions that do not fit the journal’s aims and scope, lack required information, or raise unresolved ethical concerns may be returned to authors or declined during initial editorial screening. 明显不符合本刊范围、材料不完整，或存在未解决伦理问题的稿件，可能在编辑初筛阶段被退回或拒绝。

    Publication Schedule｜出版时间安排

    Contemporary Review of Political Thought is published annually. The journal publishes one issue each year. 《当代政治思想评论》为年刊，每年出版一期。 The annual issue is scheduled for publication on 15 December each year. Accepted articles will normally be prepared, proofed, and published according to the journal’s editorial and production schedule. 本刊年度期次计划于每年 12月15日 正式出版。已录用文章通常将根据本刊编辑与制作流程完成排版、校对与出版。 The publication schedule may be adjusted when necessary due to editorial, technical, production, indexing, or other operational considerations. Any major changes to the publication schedule will be announced on the journal website. 如因编辑、技术、制作、索引准备或其他运营安排需要，本刊可对出版时间作适当调整。如出版时间发生重大变化，编辑部将在期刊网站发布公告。 Authors are encouraged to submit manuscripts in advance to allow sufficient time for editorial screening, peer review, revision, production, and proofing before the annual publication date. 本刊建议作者提前投稿，以便为编辑初审、同行评审、作者修改、排版制作与校对留出充分时间。

    Открытая инфраструктура

    Растущая открытая база данных рейтингов, рэнкингов и цитатной аналитики журналов — построенная на версионируемых, воспроизводимых данных.

    Посетить POSI →

    Открытый научный индекс Panorama (POSI) превратился в открытую базу данных доказательств на уровне журналов: рейтинги на основе жизненного цикла (AJR-E для молодых журналов, AJR-M для зрелых), тематические рейтинги по 48 категориям PSC и аналитика цитирования (PCI, PCI-5, PNCI). Каждый рейтинг, источник данных и расчёт версионируется и независимо воспроизводим — никакая оценка не выставляется закрыто.

    POSI объединяет данные Crossref, OpenAlex, OpenCitations, DOAJ, ROR и ORCID, синтезируя их в открытый для запросов индекс охвата журналов, рейтингов и влияния цитирования.
