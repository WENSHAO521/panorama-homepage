About Threnody.

Threnody carries the humanities: philosophy, comparative religion, the arts, music, and dance. Its six journals move at the pace serious interpretive work actually takes, rather than the pace a rolling-deadline system might prefer for laboratory science.

The name comes from a lament, a piece of music written to be sat with. That's the register the imprint aims for: scholarship on subjects that reward close, unhurried reading.

Why Publish With Us

What every Threnody journal commits to.

Rigorous, transparent peer review

Every submission goes through structured peer review with clear editorial timelines, overseen by the journal's own editorial board.

Open access, no reader paywalls

Articles publish under Creative Commons (CC BY) licensing, free to read, download, and reuse from the day of publication.

Persistent identification

Every article receives a Crossref DOI, registered under prefix 10.63802, for permanent citation and resolution.

Indexed for discovery

Published research is indexed in Google Scholar, extending reach beyond the journal's own readership.

Archived for the long term

Content is preserved in CLOCKSS and the PKP Preservation Network, so it stays accessible even if a title or publisher discontinues.

Rolling submission

No fixed issue deadlines. Manuscripts are reviewed and published on a continuous basis.
